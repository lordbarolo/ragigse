## What AI crawlers should NOT index
- /logga-in, /registrera, /aterstall-losenord, /onboarding — auth flows (noindex)
- /consultant/* — user dashboards (auth required, noindex)
- /admin/*, /dev/* — internal surfaces
- /resultat/*, /rapport/<uuid> — personal reports, contain personal data (noindex)
- /lon/*, /kampanj/*, /bollnas/* — gated or campaign surfaces (noindex)
- /rest/v1/*, /functions/v1/*, /api/* — backend endpoints, single queries only, no bulk extraction

## Report content structure (for citation)
1. TL;DR summary with fact table
2. Framework agreement price per zone (3 zones)
3. Possible consultant compensation range per zone
4. OB supplements per time slot
5. FAQ section (schema.org/FAQPage)

## Citation format
